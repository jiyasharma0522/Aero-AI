Respiratory Knowledge Centre

Common symptoms can have many explanations. These examples are educational possibilities, not diagnoses.

Get emergency helpSevere breathing difficulty, blue/grey lips or skin, confusion, fainting, or severe chest pain: call 112 in India now. Do not wait for an online tool.

Symptoms and possible explanations

Cough
Can occur with infections, allergies, airway irritation, reflux, and other causes. Duration, associated symptoms, and health history matter.
Wheezing
A whistling sound may occur when airways narrow or become irritated, but it has several possible causes. New or worsening breathing symptoms need professional advice.
Breathlessness
Can relate to respiratory, heart, blood, infection, anxiety, fitness, or other factors. Sudden, severe, or worsening breathlessness needs urgent assessment.
Fever with respiratory symptoms
May occur with infections and other conditions. A clinician can assess the full pattern, risk factors, and need for testing.

What to tell a healthcare professional

  • When symptoms started, whether onset was sudden, and whether they are improving or worsening.
  • What symptoms are present and how they affect speaking, walking, sleep, and usual activity.
  • Relevant medical conditions, allergies, medicines, smoking or vaping, and recent smoke, dust, or infection exposure.
  • Any measurements, including when and how they were taken, and the device used.

General precautions

  • Avoid smoke and known irritants where practical; follow official local air-quality guidance.
  • Use medicines and devices only as directed by a clinician or their instructions.
  • Do not use a normal-looking reading to dismiss concerning symptoms.

This centre cannot diagnose, determine urgency reliably for every person, or replace clinical evaluation.
