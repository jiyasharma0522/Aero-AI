Medicine categories

Short-acting bronchodilators
May relax airway muscles for some prescribed conditions. Can cause effects such as tremor or a fast heartbeat; follow the prescribed plan.
Inhaled corticosteroids
Reduce airway inflammation in certain conditions. They are generally used regularly as prescribed; rinse the mouth after use when directed by product instructions.
Antibiotics
Treat some bacterial infections, not viral illnesses. Use only when prescribed and complete the clinician-directed course.
Antihistamines
May relieve allergy symptoms. Some can cause drowsiness or interact with other medicines.
Oxygen therapy
Medical treatment prescribed for specific needs. Do not start, adjust, or borrow oxygen equipment without clinical guidance.

This is general education, not a prescription. No doses are provided. Ask a doctor or pharmacist about suitability, interactions, pregnancy, allergies, and side effects. In an emergency call 112.
