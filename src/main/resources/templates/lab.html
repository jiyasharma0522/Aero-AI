Model assumptions and limits

How to interpret this digital twin

The displayed estimate is respiratory rate multiplied by tidal volume in liters. It ignores dead space, gas exchange, mechanics, age, disease, and individual variation. Compliance is retained as an experiment parameter but does not change the estimate. This educational prototype is not a clinically validated digital twin and does not represent a real person.

Educational simulation only. No clinical decision or device control is supported. No real sensor or respiratory device is connected.
