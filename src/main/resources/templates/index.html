A transparent workflow

Learn with context at every step

Personal entries, educational models, and urgent guidance have distinct roles in the workspace.

01 · RECORD

Enter your own reading

Save a measurement with its timestamp and origin. Aero AI does not connect to a physical sensor.

02 · EXPLORE

Understand the model

Use anatomy and simulation labs to learn concepts; their scenarios are not clinical predictions.

03 · ACT SAFELY

Seek appropriate help

Use educational information as context, and contact a health professional for personal concerns.

Emergency guidance

Severe symptoms need urgent care

For severe breathing difficulty, blue or grey lips or skin, confusion, fainting, or severe chest pain in India, call 112 now. Never wait for a website or a reading.
